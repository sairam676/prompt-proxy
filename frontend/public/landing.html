The pipeline, live
01 — CONTEXT

You paste the bug. That's it.

Code, error, symptoms — whatever you've got. No template to fill out, no ticket to write.

no interview needed if enough is given
02 — EVIDENCE

It separates fact from guess.

Every claim gets labeled: directly observed in your code, or an assumption it can't confirm. Never blurred together.

✓ evidence · ? assumption
03 — CONFIDENCE

A number it actually earns.

Confidence is grounded in how much of the diagnosis rests on evidence vs. assumption — not a habit, not a guess.

self-reported, not asserted by us
04 — VERIFY

The fix gets checked, not trusted.

Syntax parsed. Packages checked against the real npm registry. No fix is "verified" on vibes.

deterministic, not another opinion
05 — REPAIR

If it's wrong, it fixes itself.

A rejected fix goes back to the same LLM with the exact failure — before you ever see something broken.

one retry, real error, no extra ask
06 — YOUR KEY

It's your LLM. Not ours.

Paste your Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, or Groq key. It runs the diagnosis on your account, at your rates — we never see or store it.
